About

Hi, welcome to my homepage, and thanks for stopping by! I am an MA student in Statistics (Machine Learning track) at Columbia University, and I received my BSc (Honours) in Financial Mathematics from the University of Liverpool and Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University in 2025. My research interest lies in extracting systematic structure from noisy, irregular transportation data, and in combining that structure with causal inference to draw insights that matter for real transportation policy. To pursue this direction, I am now deepening both the statistical foundations and the transportation domain knowledge it requires, and I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.
